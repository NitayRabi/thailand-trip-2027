Outbound & Return Flight Strategy

Flight Recommendations (AMS to Thailand)

For a family traveling with a 6-month-old infant (Lenny) and a toddler (Tom, 3y), having an assigned bassinet and low-friction flight experience is the #1 priority.

Option A: Direct Flights AMS ↔ BKK (~11h 30m)

EVA Air / KLM (Direct)

  • No Mid-Trip Transfers: Avoid waking kids up for a mid-night connection.
  • EVA Air Return Recommended: Consistently top-ranked for family hospitality and generous bassinet limits (up to 14-16kg vs KLM's 10kg limit).
  • Bassinet Strategy: Call the airline immediately after booking to secure bulkhead seats with bassinet support.
Arrival: Bangkok BKK Airport ↗
Option B: 1-Stop (1.5-2h Connection) AMS → DXB/DOH → BKK/HKT

Emirates (via Dubai) / Qatar Airways

  • Break Up the Journey: 6.5 hours to Dubai, 1.5–2h connection to stretch legs, then 6 hours to Thailand.
  • World-Class Family Amenities: Free baby strollers at Dubai (DXB) & Doha (DOH), infant amenity kits (diapers/baby food), quiet A380 cabins.
  • Flexible Arrival: Allows flying directly into Phuket (HKT) for the West Coast option without going through Bangkok first.
Hub: Dubai DXB Airport ↗

Essential Family Travel Checklist & Pro-Tips

Key advice gathered for smooth flying and effortless ground transport in Thailand.

1. Bassinet Priority

Bassinet seats (bulkhead rows) sell out fast. Immediately after flight booking, call the airline call center to lock in bassinet seats for Lenny (6 months).

2. Private Van Transfers

Pre-book private VIP vans for all airport transfers (Phuket to Khao Lak or BKK to Hua Hin). You can request infant car seats & toddler booster seats in advance.

3. Shallow Water Gear

Bring UV sunsuits and puddle-jumper floaties for Tom (3y), plus a compact travel stroller with a large sun canopy or UV cover for Lenny.
